BMW Engine Air Filter: Location and How to Replace It

By Dima · Published November 13, 2026 · Updated November 13, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

The engine air filter sits in a black airbox at the front of the engine bay. The lid is held by screws, and most BMWs have a single filter (the M car in these photos has one on each side). Take off the lid, lift out the old filter, wipe out the box, fit the new filter so it sits flat and seals all the way round, and refit the screws.

Where the air filter is

The engine air filter sits in a black plastic box, the airbox, at the front of the engine bay. In the photo, a new filter lies in front of the engine on the left, and the empty housing it goes into is on the right.

The M car in these photos has two airboxes, one on each side. Most BMWs have just one. In my experience the job is very similar on almost every BMW.

What you need

  • A screwdriver or driver bit that fits the screws on your airbox. Check the head type first
  • A new filter for your exact engine. Check the part number against your VIN in a parts catalog
  • A small vacuum or a clean cloth to wipe out the box
  • A flashlight

How to replace it, step by step

  1. Switch the engine off and let it cool, then open the hood.
  2. Undo the screws that hold the airbox lid. If a sensor or its wire is attached to the lid, don't pull on it. Disconnect it only if you have to.
  3. Lift the lid, then lift the old filter out. Note how it sits.
  4. Wipe or vacuum the inside of the box. Don't let leaves or dirt fall into the intake tube.
  5. Fit the new filter flat and check that its rubber edge seals all the way round.
  6. Refit the lid, tighten every screw and reconnect anything you disconnected.

Tip. If the new filter doesn't sit flush, don't force the lid. A gap lets unfiltered air into the engine. Re-seat the filter or check that it is the right part.

How often to replace it

Follow your service schedule, and replace it sooner if you drive on dusty roads or the filter is visibly dirty. On a modern fuel-injected engine a dirty filter rarely causes dramatic symptoms, so don't wait for them. Its main job is to protect the engine.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving the lid loosely closed or a screw out
  • Dropping debris into the intake
  • Pulling on sensor wires
  • Blowing the filter out with compressed air instead of replacing it
  • Fitting a filter that is not for your engine

FAQ

Where is the engine air filter on a BMW?

In a black airbox at the front of the engine bay. Most cars have one, and some M models have one on each side.

How is the airbox held shut?

By screws, on the cars I work with. Check the head type before you start.

Does a BMW have one air filter or two?

Most have one. The M car in these photos has two, one on each side.

How often should the engine air filter be changed?

Follow your service schedule and replace it sooner if it is dirty or you drive in dust.

Can I clean and reuse the filter?

Tapping off loose dirt is the most I would do. A properly dirty filter should be replaced.
