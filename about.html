About ServiceBay

I'm Dima. I work at a BMW service, and I started ServiceBay to write down the things owners ask me most: where a part is, what to check and what not to ignore.

I'm still learning the trade, so I'm more of a trainee than a senior mechanic. Over the last two years I've been doing routine services myself, including brakes and shock absorbers. I'm not a master technician and I don't pretend to be. The guides are written from the point of view of someone who is still learning: I explain what I understand, say clearly when I'm unsure and point you to the manufacturer's manual for exact specifications.

What you'll find here

Practical guides about where things are on a BMW and how to check them: battery and electrical, fluids and filters, brakes and suspension, and everyday diagnostics. Locations and procedures can differ between model generations, so I say which car a photo or a step applies to.

How I write

  • Photos are my own unless stated otherwise.
  • I don't publish customer details, license plates, vehicle identification numbers or internal workshop documents.
  • Where safety matters, I add a clear safety note instead of burying it.
  • If I'm not sure about something, I say so and point you to the manufacturer's information.
  • Nothing is copied from other sites.
