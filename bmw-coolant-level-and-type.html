BMW Coolant: How to Check the Level and Which Type to Use

By Dima · Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 14, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

Check the coolant only when the engine is completely cold, by looking at the level marks in the expansion tank. Top up only with the coolant type your car calls for. At the service I use two different kinds, a dark blue one and a light green one, and they are not interchangeable. After topping up, close the cap tightly, because the system is under pressure.

Where the coolant tank is

The coolant sits in a translucent expansion tank under the hood, with a cap that has a yellow warning symbol on it. The photo shows one. On the M car in What's Under the Hood of a BMW, coolant goes in at two separate places, so look at your own engine bay.

How to check the level

  1. Let the engine cool completely. Several hours is best, so check it first thing in the morning.
  2. Park on level ground and open the hood.
  3. Look at the level marks. On this car the MIN and MAX marks are visible inside the filler neck.
  4. If the level is low, top it up with the correct coolant, to MAX and not above.

Safety note. Never open the coolant cap on a hot engine. The system is pressurized and can spray scalding coolant. Antifreeze is toxic, so keep it away from children and pets.

Which coolant to use

Not every coolant is the same. At the service I use two kinds, a dark blue one and a light green one. Which one your car needs depends on the model and year, so check your manual or ask a shop, and don't choose by color alone. Never mix different types, and top up only with the type your car calls for.

Close the cap properly

After you check or top up, the cap must be closed well. The coolant system is under pressure, and a loose cap lets coolant and pressure escape, which can lead to overheating.

Signs the coolant is low

  • The temperature warning comes on or the gauge climbs
  • The heater blows cold air
  • A sweet smell from the engine bay
  • Puddles under the car
  • A level that keeps falling

Coolant isn't used up. A falling level means it is going somewhere, so find the leak instead of just topping up.

FAQ

Can I use any coolant in my BMW?

No. Use the type your car calls for, and never mix different types. At the service I use two kinds, dark blue and light green.

When should I check the coolant?

Only when the engine is completely cold. Checking it about once a month is a sensible habit.

Why does my coolant level keep dropping?

Coolant isn't used up, so there is a leak or another problem. Have it checked.

Can I top up with water?

Only as a short-term emergency measure. Water dilutes the protection against freezing and corrosion.

Does the color tell me which coolant to buy?

Not reliably. Use your manual or the specification on the bottle.
