BMW Tire Pressure: Where to Find the Right PSI

By Dima · Published November 14, 2026 · Updated November 14, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

The tire pressure sticker is on the side of the door opening. It lists pressures for different tire sizes and loads, so read the row for your exact tire size carefully. Check the pressure when the tires are cold, using a reliable gauge, and set each tire to the figure for your car.

Where to find the pressure

The pressure is printed on a sticker on the side of the door opening, on the door frame. Your owner's manual lists it too. The number on the sidewall of the tire is the maximum, not the recommended pressure.

Read the sticker carefully

The sticker often covers several tire sizes and loads in one table, so read it carefully:

  • Find the row for your exact tire size. It is printed on the tire sidewall
  • Check whether the figures are for normal or heavy loads
  • Front and rear tires can have different pressures
  • Note the unit. It may be shown in bar, kPa or psi

How to check it

  1. Check when the tires are cold, before driving or after the car has stood for a few hours.
  2. Remove the valve cap and press the gauge on firmly.
  3. Compare the reading with the sticker and add or release air in small steps.
  4. Refit the valve caps and check all four tires.

After you change the pressure

Many BMWs have a tire pressure monitoring system. After you adjust the pressures or change a wheel, your car may ask you to confirm or reset it in the menu, so check your manual. If your car has run-flat tires, a low tire can look almost normal, so don't rely on how it looks.

Common mistakes

  • Using the sidewall number instead of the sticker
  • Reading the wrong row for the tire size
  • Checking hot tires
  • Forgetting to reset the monitoring system when the manual says to

FAQ

Where is the tire pressure on a BMW?

On a sticker on the side of the door opening. The manual lists it too.

Is the pressure the same on the front and rear?

Not always. Read the row for your tire size, and check the front and rear figures.

Is the number on the tire the pressure I should use?

No. It is the maximum the tire can take. Use the sticker.

Do I need to reset the tire pressure monitor?

Many BMWs ask you to confirm or reset it after you change the pressure or the wheels. Check your manual.

Should I check the pressure hot or cold?

Cold. Hot readings are higher and can mislead you.
