Safety first

Vehicle work can involve heavy loads, hot or pressurized components, brake and fuel systems, airbags, chemicals and electrical systems. Hybrid and electric models contain high-voltage components that can cause serious injury or death: never work on them unless you are trained and authorized. Use proper jack stands, wear protective equipment, work in a ventilated space and follow the manufacturer's procedures. If you are not comfortable with a job, or the car is your only transport and the job affects braking, steering or suspension, have it done by a professional.
